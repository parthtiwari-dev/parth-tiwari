04 / What failed

The previous frame remembered too much.

Conditioning on rendered pixels copied its hand pose into the next composition. The mark shown here is illustrative; a publishable failure export is still required.

05 / Bind

Keep the identity. Let the shot move.

The intended end state binds named characters to neutral references instead of chaining finished frames.

Anime, watercolour, ink and stop-motion are direction studies—not current FLUX.2 capability claims. They ship only after real compatible outputs exist.
