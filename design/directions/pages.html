One signature, three supporting layers

The signature

The fused denoise-and-settle. Grain clears while the variable font resolves from wide and light to tight and heavy, on one curve. It happens once, on arrival, on the home page only. No other page has it.

The base

Hierarchy from scale, not weight. Body at 200. Zero cards, zero borders on content, zero shadows. One accent, and it only ever marks the thing meant to be clicked.

The substrate

Every claim carries a source. Invisible until touched, so it costs nothing visually and it makes an unsourced line conspicuous, which is the point.

Non-negotiable

Four rules the signature has to obey

  1. The headline is real text, readable before the effect starts. Never gated behind it. The static first frame is a complete page on its own, which is the award-grade standard, not a compromise against it.
  2. Under 1.2 seconds, once per session. Held in sessionStorage, not replayed on every scroll into view. An arrival effect that fires repeatedly becomes a toll booth.
  3. Reduced motion and no-JS both get the resolved frame immediately. The final state, not a shortened animation.
  4. The canvas is never a control. Nothing is clicked through it, no content lives inside it, no legend explains it. That is the exact line v1 crossed.
