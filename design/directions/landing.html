Notes

What I got wrong.

Every developer claims things went well. This is the other half, kept because it is the only part that is hard to fake. No posting schedule. It grows when something breaks.

02 Aug 2026

A checkbox is not a rights check.

I shipped a self-attestation tickbox for copyright, then deleted it. Export now has to resolve a track's full lineage across two independent sources, and anything unresolvable blocks. The tickbox would have moved liability onto the user while doing nothing about the problem.

19 Jul 2026

I predicted forty percent. I measured eight.

A model swap that was supposed to be a large win was not, so it went back. The measurement is the useful artifact, not the hypothesis. I keep the prediction next to the result so the gap stays visible.

28 Jun 2026

I declined to bypass the CAPTCHA.

An automation I was building hit an invisible reCAPTCHA. Getting around it was possible. I shipped assisted review instead, because the version that worked would have been the version I could not describe to a client.

11 Jun 2026

Conditioning each shot on the last one made it worse.

It was supposed to preserve continuity. At the guidance I was using, the previous frame's hand pose overrode the text prompt, and the growing reference chain made later shots roughly four times slower. A single character anchor fixed both.
