Phase 5 / BeatMind Production storyboard lock 2026-09-01

Selected treatment / Precision Descent

One song descends through one machine.

Native vertical scroll follows a single specimen from source waveform to five stems, analysis, arrangement and mixdown. The machinery creates atmosphere. Only exported BeatMind data creates the signal.

Treatment decision

Three directions, one production choice.

Selected

Precision Descent

A persistent machine and a vertical camera journey. It keeps the cause-and-effect chain legible, gives the phone its own close crops and needs only ordinary scrolling.

Rejected

Foundry Conveyor

A lateral production line makes the machinery easy to stage on desktop, but weakens the phone composition and encourages captured horizontal scrolling.

Rejected

Signal Autopsy

An evidence-first dissection is clear and economical, but loses too much of the immersive machine that makes BeatMind feel like a world rather than a diagram.

Production sequence

The machine only shows what the export can prove.

  1. 01

    Specimen chamber

    One complete source waveform enters on one shared clock.

  2. 02

    Separation gantry

    The source becomes vocals, backing vocals, drums, bass and other.

  3. 03

    Worker failure

    Included only when an explicitly correlated real lifecycle trace proves it.

  4. 04

    Retry

    Included only with the same verified trace. Never inferred from timestamps.

  5. 05

    Analysis scanner

    Real sections, downbeats, BPM, key and permitted analysis labels appear.

  6. 06

    Arrangement deck

    The five connected lanes become a readable section arrangement.

  7. 07

    Mix chamber

    Shared-scale envelopes stay visible while the lanes converge.

  8. 08

    Render press

    Five lanes resolve into one mixdown representation.

  9. 09

    Measurement and boundary

    Benchmark context, denominator, limits, omitted trace and withheld audio are stated plainly.

  10. 10

    Deep-dive handoff

    One action opens the canonical paper case study.

Non-negotiable boundary

No export, no signal.

  • No invented waveform, marker, duration or trace.
  • No audio element until a cleared excerpt and licence record exist.
  • No failure scene when correlation cannot be proven.
  • No trapped scroll, runtime fetch or required interaction.
