Calling Tathya a fact checker created pressure for a score and a final answer. That made
the interface feel decisive while the source record could still be partial or conflicting.

The product is now framed as a sourced record. It can group claims, preserve citations,
and show disagreement. It stops before ranking publishers or telling the reader what to
believe.
