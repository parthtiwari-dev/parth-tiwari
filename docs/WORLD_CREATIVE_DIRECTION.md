## What the owner is asking for

Anime-influenced realism, with Suzume offered as a reference: illustrated environments,
believable depth, expressive light, designed shapes and animation. This is not a request
to copy its characters, locations or specific shots. The user wants movement tied to
scroll and actual world-building, not a photoreal background with zoom and floating UI.
Each project's composition, motion and central interaction must be different.

Order Supervisor is a positive reference: a persistent object changes mechanically and
its behavior explains the project's boundary. SecondSelf's room is liked, but its story
must start from the intention to behave like the owner when searching and preparing for
jobs. Human review remains the actual implementation boundary, not its entire identity.
MedRAG remains the weak direction. Explore the illustrated theatre premise further.

## Diagnosis

The current MedRAG and SecondSelf share a presentation template: left narrative column,
one full-screen photograph, scene-wise zoom, floating objects and a small readout.
Different images do not make different experiences. More photoreal assets or longer
paragraphs will not fix that. Need storyboards and independently animated layers first.

## Proposed MedRAG experiment: The Theatre of an Answer

Build one opening and one refusal sequence as an alternate, not another complete world.
An illustrated theatre is the visual metaphor; the facts remain the same two records.

- Arrival: close curtains, low footlights, readable question. The first scroll parts the
  curtains and reveals a stage, rather than simply enlarging a background.
- Retrieval: eight distinct scenic pieces enter from wings and fly-lines. Their numbering,
  if used, is editorial, not actual chunk identity. The camera changes from auditorium
  view to a lateral/backstage composition.
- Cited result: three citation marks stay visibly attached to the represented answer.
  No answer wording or inference mechanism is invented.
- Second query: the eight retrieved pieces return, so the visitor recognizes equal counts.
- Refusal: the stage is ready, but the answer never takes the centre. The curtains open
  wider to reveal the absence. Refusal is the climax rather than a failure screen.
- Optional inspection: compare the two records by moving between their stage arrangements.
  Avoid turning source counts into unsupported similarity scores or medical conclusions.

Art: painterly backgrounds, drawn architecture, shaped light, independently moving
curtains/rigging/props and a few carefully authored effects. Full-stage shots intercut with
short narration; not eight copies of a two-column hero. No full-frame photographic base.

## Proposed SecondSelf evolution: a companion on the search

Preserve the room. Reframe the journey around a job-search companion working from the
owner's evidence. Distinguish current capabilities from the ambition to behave like them.

- The room opens into an illustrated landscape of opportunities, not a repeated desk zoom.
- Two synchronized marks or silhouettes represent the owner and their assistant. No
  invented physical likeness or private personal record is needed.
- The assistant follows recorded experience, gathers candidate material, and discards
  unsupported claims before preparing the packet.
- Perspective returns to the room for review. The companion can prepare the next step;
  the final decision remains visibly the person's.
- Interactions inspect support and rehearse review; no fabricated matches, offers, success
  rates, personal preferences or actual application submissions.

Movement grammar: side-on travel, mirrored actions and handoffs. MedRAG uses theatrical
reveals; Order Supervisor uses mechanical transforms. Only lifecycle plumbing is shared.

## Finite next step

Recommend a small alternate MedRAG motion proof, with opening and refusal, before changing
all worlds. Judge it in motion on desktop and phone. If it clearly improves the story,
use the lessons to evolve SecondSelf and lock remaining world briefs. If not, keep the
saved builds. Do not expand into an endless style exploration or claim unprecedentedness.

A strong experience is achievable; no one can establish that nobody has ever built
anything similar or guarantee every visitor's response. Use concrete quality checks:
memorable opening, legible project thesis, distinct motion, consequential interaction,
clear ending, accessible fallback, and responsive performance.
